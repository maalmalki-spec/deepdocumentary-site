Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Deep Documentary respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is accessed, used, stored, and protected when using Deep Documentary services and integrations.

Google and YouTube API Services

Deep Documentary uses Google OAuth 2.0 and YouTube Data API services to support authorized YouTube channel management and video publishing operations.

When Google authorization is used, the application may request permission to upload videos and manage authorized YouTube account resources on behalf of the account owner.

Information We Access

Depending on the permissions granted by the user, the application may access information necessary to identify the authorized YouTube channel, upload videos, retrieve video processing or status information, and manage video publishing settings.

How Information Is Used

Information obtained through Google or YouTube APIs is used only to provide the requested channel management and video publishing functionality. It is not sold to third parties or used for advertising.

Data Storage and Security

Authorization credentials and related application data are protected using reasonable technical and organizational security measures. Access is limited to authorized systems and users as necessary to operate the service.

Data Sharing

Deep Documentary does not sell, rent, or trade Google user data. Information may only be shared when necessary to provide the requested service, comply with applicable law, or protect the security and integrity of the service.

Revoking Google Account Access

Users may revoke the application's access to their Google Account at any time through their Google Account security settings.

Google account permissions can be reviewed at Google Account Third-Party Connections .

Data Retention and Deletion

Authorization credentials and related data are retained only for as long as necessary to provide the authorized functionality. Users may revoke Google access at any time. When access is no longer required, stored authorization credentials may be deleted from the application's systems.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Deep Documentary's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes in the service, applicable requirements, or privacy practices.

Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the official Deep Documentary website.
