# Deep Documentary — Mandatory Bilingual Cover Standard

Applies to **every published article**, all future articles, and homepage featured/cards.

1. Create TWO dedicated 1600×900 or larger covers: `assets/covers/<article-slug>-ar.svg` and `assets/covers/<article-slug>-en.svg` (or high-resolution locally stored WebP/PNG).
2. The Arabic cover uses Arabic editorial headline; English cover uses English editorial headline. Never use a tiny favicon, pixelated thumbnail, remote hotlink, or misleading mock screenshot as an article cover.
3. Preserve the cinematic black/gold Deep Documentary brand with distinct subject-relevant imagery. The cover is an **editorial illustration**, never represented as a historical photograph or real app screenshot.
4. In `index.html`, set `image` to the Arabic cover and `imageEn` to the English cover. Both cards and the featured image MUST use the language-dependent image.
5. Add both covers to the article page and update image on Arabic/English language switch. Validate each URL, typography, legibility and mobile cropping before release.
6. Tutorial **step images** are separate from cover illustrations: prefer genuine, current software screenshots with verified source/rights. No imaginary interface screenshot.
7. Store locally, record provenance/rights in SOURCES.json, and verify published pages after deployment.

Adopted November 2026. No editorial approval required for individual covers.
