## Primary and institutional sources
1. Parks Canada — 1845 expedition: https://www.parcs.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nu/epaveswrecks/culture/histoire-history/expedition
   - Departed Greenhithe 19 May 1845 with 134; 129 remained after July 1845 departure from Greenland.
   - Wintered Beechey Island 1845–46; icebound September 1846; ships abandoned 22 April 1848.
2. Parks Canada — search history and Victory Point record: https://www.parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nu/epaveswrecks/culture/histoire-history/suivant-next
   - 1847 first message said 'all well'; 1848 addition reported Franklin died 11 June 1847 and 24 men dead; Crozier and Fitzjames signed.
3. Gonville & Caius College, Cambridge — transcription of the 1848 message: https://www.cai.cam.ac.uk/discover/library/online-exhibitions/safe-return-doubtful/search-franklin
   - 105 survivors abandoned vessels 22 April 1848. Cite the dated document, not a fictional first-person scene.
